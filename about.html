About ArthaDarsana

A platform for examining economics, ethics and society through philosophical inquiry.

A vision of economic thought beyond economics alone.

ArthaDarsana is an independent research-oriented platform exploring the relationship between economics, ethics, society and philosophical thought.

It examines ideas concerning wealth, desire, prosperity, responsibility and human well-being through philosophical and literary inquiry, bringing them into dialogue with contemporary questions.

The platform begins with research papers. Over time, selected inquiries may develop into books and other publications.
